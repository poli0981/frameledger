# Names and trademarks

FrameLedger measures games on hardware, drivers and platforms made by other companies, so its software and its
documents name their products constantly. This page says how those names are used, and how the name *FrameLedger*
itself may be used. It is information for readers of the repository and of the documentation; it is not one of the
documents the app asks you to accept.

## The name FrameLedger

*FrameLedger* is the name of this project. The licence (`LICENSE`, `NOTICE`) gives you broad rights in the software
but, by its additional term (e), **no rights in the name**, the project's logo or its icon, beyond describing truthfully
where a work comes from:

- **fine:** "based on FrameLedger", "a fork of FrameLedger", "compatible with FrameLedger's export format", a link to
  the project, an article or video about FrameLedger;
- **not fine:** naming a modified version "FrameLedger", using the icon or name for a build the project did not make,
  or suggesting the developer made or endorses something they did not (NOTICE (c)).

`FORKING.md` says what a fork that ships builds should rename.
